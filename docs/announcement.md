## Introducing Feed the Flock

Introducing **Feed the Flock**, an Omarchy plugin and companion to Herdr for those of us who find ourselves sitting around, coming up with feedback and new prompts faster than an agent can handle them.

You do not want to type everything into the session at once. When several unrelated messages get dumped into steering within a minute, they can pull the session in every direction.

Feed the Flock lets you keep notes in project-specific buckets, organize them into sections, and manage them easily. It can automatically feed each section to your Herdr sessions as your agents become idle, giving every prompt room to be handled in order.

Of course, you can still interact with your sessions exactly as before. And when something cannot wait, you can still drop that message into steering immediately with **Feed Now**.
