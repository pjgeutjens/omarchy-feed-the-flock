### Maintainer notes

Feed the Flock uses commands included with current Omarchy Quattro. It can turn a capture from the optional Omarchy Dictation app into a note; Dictation itself is separate, and typed notes and Herdr delivery work without it. The workspace listens only on 127.0.0.1. User-configured global shortcuts are managed in a separate file, and conflicting shortcuts require explicit approval before an override is added. The removal helper stops background work and removes that managed keybinding integration. Stored notes and attachments remain until the user deletes them.
